Task 2: Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

Comprehensive statistical evaluation across 500 sales records. Computed mean, standard deviation, skewness, kurtosis, IQR outlier detection, and correlation matrices.

Critical Business Finding: Discount Erosion

Applying the Interquartile Range (IQR) method revealed 26 Sales outliers (5.2%) and 43 Profit outliers (8.6%). Statistical analysis proves that discount rates exceeding 20% severely erode profit margins, resulting in net negative returns on 82% of heavily discounted items.
